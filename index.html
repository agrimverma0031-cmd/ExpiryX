Automated Email & Phone Alerts

Browser notifications only work when your browser is open. To receive real Email, SMS, or Telegram alerts automatically when you are away, connect a webhook endpoint (such as Ntfy.sh, Make.com, Zapier, or Discord Webhook).

Tip: Use free services like ntfy.sh to send instant push notifications directly to your iOS or Android phone without writing code.

Free & Simple Alert Integrations:

  • 1. Phone Push via Ntfy: Enter https://ntfy.sh/your_custom_topic_name and install the free Ntfy app on your phone.
  • 2. Discord / Slack: Paste your Discord channel Webhook URL to get direct notifications in your Discord server.
  • 3. Zapier / Make (Email/SMS): Connect a webhook trigger to send emails or SMS messages automatically.
